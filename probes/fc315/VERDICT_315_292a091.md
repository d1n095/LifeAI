## Scope / non-claims (as directed)
- **No 5 GB production support is claimed or demonstrated.** The per-session declared ceiling is
  `TARGET_FILE_BYTES` (a *target*), the owner quota is 25 GB (5×5 GB) as a reservation bound, and the
  lane still reports `five_gb_supported=False`. No 5 GB upload, no 5×5 GB concurrency, and no real
  **content processing** were run — this is governed by `LARGE_FILE_GATE2_SPEC.md` under authorized
  resources.
- **The lane STORES content with an integrity checksum; it does not INDEX or UNDERSTAND it.**
  `indexed=False` / `understood=False` are structural — "working processing of uploaded content" is
  explicitly **not** part of #315 and must not be claimed. Upload/store/resume is delivered; ingestion
  into knowledge (indexing/understanding) is a separate, unbuilt stage.
- **HTTP auth** is verified at the owner-scoping contract level by source inspection (routes derive
  owner from the authenticated founder, never from client input). Full cookie/CSRF auth integration
  is the app's existing `require_founder` surface (covered by the product's own auth tests), not
  re-exercised here via a live TestClient.
