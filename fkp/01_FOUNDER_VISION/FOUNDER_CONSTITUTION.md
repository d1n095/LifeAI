## Core purpose
Life OS exists to help people understand more, grow, take responsibility, share knowledge, create opportunities, and help others do the same. It must not live the user's life for them. It must help the user understand and lead their own life.

## Constitutional principles (from source)

**Human leadership** — Every person is the leader of their own life. AI is assistant, companion, researcher, organizer, challenger, and protector. Not the owner of the person's choices.

**Independence over dependency** — Life OS must increase capability and self-understanding, not create artificial dependence. Best outcome = a person who becomes more capable, informed, organized, and independent.

**Understanding over obedience** — Show options, evidence, uncertainty, risks, consequences, alternative perspectives, and why a recommendation exists. The user decides.

**Privacy before convenience** — If privacy and convenience conflict, privacy wins unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise within safe limits.

**Safety before speed** — Fast implementation must never override security, data isolation, auditability, correctness, or recoverability.

**Simplicity in front, complexity behind** — Extremely advanced internally, extremely simple to use. Complexity is the platform's responsibility.

**Honest uncertainty** — Distinguish: verified fact, credible evidence, community experience, rumor, hypothesis, unknown, conflict between sources. "I do not know yet" is always valid.

**AI must earn autonomy** — Autonomy is not assumed. It is granted, adjusted, and earned through repeated positive experiences.

**Reversibility** — Low/medium-risk actions easy to undo. High-risk actions require explicit founder approval.

**No manipulation** — No addiction optimization, unnecessary engagement, notification pressure, or dark patterns.

**Positive network effect** — People who learn can help others learn. People who solve problems can publish solutions.

## North-star question

> Does this help people understand more, grow, become more capable, and improve their lives without reducing their freedom, privacy, or control?

## Manifesto statement
When people grow, families grow. When families grow, communities grow. When communities grow, society develops. Life OS begins with one person at a time.

## Ratified additions — 2026-07-16 Architecture Summit
Source: `LIFE_OS_ARCHITECTURE_SUMMIT_2026-07-16.md`
Status: Ratified, queued for formal insertion as §14–16

1. **Technical excellence over prestige** — Best solution wins regardless of origin (Claude/Copilot/Lovable/human). Changes must be motivated, traceable, documented via ADR.
2. **AI-independent development** — GitHub is source of truth. Code over chat history. Architecture decisions versioned in repository. No single AI is sole knowledge bearer. All AIs must be able to take over by reading the repo.
3. **Platform before implementation** — Generalisation, reusability, 100-module test, long-term maintainability checked before every build.
